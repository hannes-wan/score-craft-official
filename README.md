## Why Score Craft exists

Score Craft is an offline desktop sheet music scanner, audition player, and notation proofreading workbench built for pianists, arrangers, teachers, and music students on Windows x64 and Linux x64.

> **Edition boundary:** The technical sections below describe the complete product surface and can include optional licensed workflows. The exact free Community Edition scope is listed separately below.

### Built for the messy bars that break automatic scanners

Three places where local recognition, live audio audition, and side-by-side PDF evidence work together.

### Scan PDFs and images locally

Recognize multi-page PDF sheet music, PNG or JPG scans, and existing MusicXML or MXL files on Windows and Linux with zero cloud uploads.

### Audition with live playback

Hear full grand-staff piano and multi-part scores through Salamander Grand piano plus six built-in audition voices, continuous BPM speed control, and a synchronized score map.

### Proofread against source crops

Inspect pinpoint diagnostics by bar, hand, and note pitch with cropped original PDF excerpts, one-click Locate and Audition controls, and targeted structural fixes.

### Edit measures and export clean files

Adjust pitches, accidentals, note values, ties, keys, meters, and tempos with full Undo and Redo, then export MusicXML, MIDI, or WAV audio.

### Catch faint beams and beat mismatches in minutes

- The score map highlights flagged measures across the entire piece so you never scan fifty clean bars looking for one missing flag.
- Each diagnostic card crops the exact bar from the source PDF with a blue target box and plays the measure on demand before you apply a fix.

### Practice and verify with realistic piano sound

- Audition grand-staff piano and multi-part scores through the built-in Salamander Grand piano and six additional audition voices.
- Adjust continuous BPM playback speed to expose wrong accidentals, dangling ties, or octave jumps that look plausible on screen but sound wrong immediately.

### Keep repertoire offline and own your exports

- Run recognition, proofreading, notation editing, and playback free in Community Edition without creating an account or uploading unpublished scores.
- Unlock MusicXML, compressed MXL, MIDI, and rendered WAV exports with a Website Direct license (Professional or Team) or Microsoft Store unlock.

## Frequently asked questions

> **Community scope:** Community Edition keeps PDF/photo scanning, pinpoint proofreading, notation editing, and playback free; exporting edited MusicXML, MIDI, and WAV audio requires a licensed edition.

<details>
<summary><strong>Does Score Craft upload my PDF sheet music to the cloud?</strong></summary>

No. Score Craft runs optical recognition, score engraving, proofreading, editing, and piano playback entirely offline on your own Windows or Linux machine. Unpublished arrangements, exam pieces, and personal PDF libraries never leave your local drive.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Which sheet music file formats can Score Craft open?</strong></summary>

Score Craft opens multi-page PDF sheet music, single or appended PNG and JPG scanned pages, and existing MusicXML (.musicxml) or compressed MXL (.mxl) scores. You can also append additional image or PDF pages from the Reference Pages dock.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How does the Studio Proofreading Dock help fix scanning errors?</strong></summary>

Each diagnostic pinpoints the exact bar, hand, pitch, and beat discrepancy alongside a zoomed crop of the original PDF marked with a blue target box. Click Locate or Audition to hear the bar, apply one-click structural fixes like Auto-balance bar or Clear dangling tie, and mark items resolved.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I edit rhythms, accidentals, key signatures, and meters inside Score Craft?</strong></summary>

Yes. Click any note or rest to shift semitones or octaves, apply flat, natural, or sharp accidentals, change durations from 1/1 to 1/32 with dots, toggle ties, add third intervals, enter pitches by letter name, or update bar tempo, meter, key signature, clef, and repeat barlines.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Which Score Craft installer should I download for Windows or Linux?</strong></summary>

On Windows 10 or later (x64), download the official Windows x64 installer (.exe) directly from the download page or install Score Craft from Microsoft Store. On Linux x64, choose DEB, RPM, or portable AppImage. Every direct download publishes its SHA-256 checksum.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I open exported MusicXML or MIDI files in MuseScore, Dorico, Sibelius, or a DAW?</strong></summary>

Yes. Score Craft exports standards-compliant MusicXML (.musicxml) and compressed MXL (.mxl) for desktop notation editors such as MuseScore, Dorico, Sibelius, and Finale, plus standard MIDI (.mid) for DAWs and rendered WAV (.wav) for practice playback.

</details>
